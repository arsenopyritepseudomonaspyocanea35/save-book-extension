## 👋 Welcome

Do you ever get tired of typing your login details, phone numbers, or secret codes over and over? Do you have a sticky note on your monitor with important info? This extension replaces that mess with a neat, tiny card that pops up right where you need it.

Save-book-extension is a Chrome extension that pins a small, draggable card of your saved logins, codes, and phone numbers to the websites you choose. No servers, no tracking, no accounts. Everything stays in your browser. It is private, simple, and fast.

## 📖 How to Use

Using save-book-extension is just as simple as installing it.

### Adding Your First Item

1. Click on the extension icon in your toolbar.
2. A small popup will appear. Click the "Add New" button.
3. Type in a label (like "Work Email Password" or "Home WiFi Code").
4. Type in the actual value (the password, code, or number).
5. Choose a website from the dropdown list, or type a new website address (like `example.com`).
6. Click "Save".

### Pinning a Card to a Site

1. Go to the website you selected (for example, `example.com`).
2. The extension will automatically show a small card on the screen with your saved info.
3. You can click and drag the card anywhere you like. Its position will be remembered for that site.

### Copying Information Quickly

When you need to use the saved info, just click on the value inside the card. It will be copied to your clipboard instantly. Then you can paste it into any login field or form.

### Managing Your Saved Items

Click the extension icon again to see a list of all your saved items. From there, you can edit, delete, or re-pin items as needed.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is my data safe?
Yes. Your data never leaves your computer. There is no server, no database, no network requests. Even if your computer is stolen, your data is encrypted at the browser level and only accessible after you unlock your Chrome profile.

### Will this work on any website?
It works on any HTTP or HTTPS website. You can pin a card to any domain.

### Can I have multiple cards on the same site?
Yes, you can add as many items as you want for a single site. They will all appear on the same draggable card.

### What happens if I clear my browser data?
If you clear your browsing data in Chrome, you may also delete the extension’s saved data. Be careful. To be safe, you can back up your data by exporting it (if you have that feature enabled) or simply re-entering it.

### Is there a mobile version?
This is a Chrome extension, so it only works in the Chrome browser on desktop or laptop computers. There is no mobile app.
